# Code of Conduct

MailLayer is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone. Be constructive, assume good intent, and focus
feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
attention without consent, doxxing, and publishing private information.
Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.

Report concerns privately through the security or repository contact channels.
